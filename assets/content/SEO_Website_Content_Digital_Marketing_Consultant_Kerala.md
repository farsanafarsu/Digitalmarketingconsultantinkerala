You have 4 years against their 8 to 10. Don't compete on years. Compete on platform range, education (BSc Computer Science, MCA) and numbers.

## On-page SEO checklist and schema

On-page work gets you into the contest. Ranking first also depends on backlinks, a Google Business Profile, reviews and time, so no page copy can promise position one.

- [ ] Primary keyword in the title tag, H1, first 100 words, one H2 and one image alt text
- [ ] Exact keyword used 5 to 7 times on the homepage, with variations elsewhere. No stuffing
- [ ] One H1 per page, then H2 and H3 in order
- [ ] Fill every square bracket with real facts before publishing
- [ ] Images in WebP, under 150 KB, with descriptive file names such as `digital-marketing-consultant-kerala.webp`
- [ ] Mobile page speed: Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5 seconds
- [ ] Internal links from the homepage to every service page and back
- [ ] WhatsApp button, phone number and email in the header and footer
- [ ] Name, address and phone written identically on the site and Google Business Profile
- [ ] XML sitemap submitted in Google Search Console, and `robots.txt` allowing GPTBot, PerplexityBot and Google-Extended so AI tools can read the site
- [ ] An `llms.txt` file summarising who you are and what you offer
- [ ] Create a Google Business Profile and ask your first clients for reviews
- [ ] Publish two blog posts a month on Kerala-specific topics, such as "Google Ads cost in Kerala" or "How much does SEO cost in Kochi"
