The checklist

Six things to check before you hire.

Ask these of any consultant or agency in Kerala. A good one will answer each without hesitating.

1. Can they show real results?

Ask for case studies with screenshots from Ads Manager or Search Console, not a slide with big round numbers. Results in a business like yours count for more than a famous logo.

2. Do they track enquiries, not likes?

Reach and followers are easy to buy. Ask how they will count calls, WhatsApp chats and form fills, and what each one cost you.

3. Who does the actual work?

With many agencies, the person who pitches is not the person who runs your account. Ask who will set up your campaigns and who you can call when something breaks.

4. Will you own your accounts?

Your ad accounts, Business Manager, Search Console and analytics should be in your name. If a consultant runs them from their own account, you lose your history when you leave.

5. Do they promise rankings or lead counts?

No one controls Google or Meta. A consultant who guarantees page one or a fixed number of leads is guessing. Look for someone who commits to the work and reports honestly.

6. Do they understand Kerala buyers?

People here search in English and Malayalam, ask for prices on WhatsApp and check Google reviews before they visit. Your consultant should plan for that.
