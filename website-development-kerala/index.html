The short answer

What does website development include?

Website development is planning, designing, building, testing and launching a site, then keeping it secure and up to date. For a business, it should also cover search and enquiry tracking.

Plan and write

The pages you need, the words on them and the one action each visitor should take.

Design and build

A mobile-first design, fast code, forms, WhatsApp and the tools your business uses.

Test and look after

Speed, links, forms, search settings and tracking checked before launch, and care afterwards.

One job

A business website has one job.

Turn a visitor into an enquiry. Everything else, the colours, the animations, the photos, should serve that job or get out of the way.

In practice that means a page that loads in a couple of seconds on a phone, says clearly what you do and where, shows proof, and puts call, WhatsApp and a short form within one tap. Small things, but together they can turn the same traffic into several times more enquiries.

FAQ

Questions owners ask about websites in Kerala.

What does a website developer do?

A website developer plans, builds, tests and launches a website. I also cover the copy, mobile design, search basics, enquiry forms and tracking, so the site is ready to bring customers.

Who is the best web designer in Kerala for a small business?

The best one for you puts the domain and hosting in your name, shows live sites you can test on a phone, includes SEO and tracking in the build and stays available after launch.

Should I use WordPress or a custom website?

Choose WordPress if your team will edit pages or publish posts often. Choose a hand-built site if you want fast service or landing pages that change rarely. Either can rank on Google when built well.

How much does a website cost in Kerala?

It depends on how many pages you need, who writes the content, how custom the design is, and what it must connect to, such as WhatsApp, forms, booking or a CRM. I quote a fixed price after a short call, so you know the full cost before work starts.

How long does it take to build a website?

A focused landing page usually takes one to two weeks. A business website of five to ten pages usually takes three to five weeks, mostly depending on how quickly the content and photos are ready.

Will my website work well on mobile?

Yes. Most people in Kerala will see your site on a phone, often on mobile data, so I design and test for phones first, then adapt for larger screens.

Will the website be SEO-ready?

Yes. Clean headings, page titles and descriptions, a sitemap, structured data, fast loading and proper redirects from any old pages are part of every build, not an add-on.

Can you add WhatsApp, call and enquiry forms?

Yes. Tap-to-call, WhatsApp buttons and enquiry forms are set up on every site, and form enquiries can be sent to your email, WhatsApp or a Google Sheet.

Do you set up Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel?

Yes. Analytics 4, Tag Manager, the Meta Pixel and Conversions API are installed and tested, so when you run ads you can see exactly which ones bring enquiries.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. I start by checking what already ranks and brings enquiries, keep what works, and redirect old pages properly so you don’t lose your Google rankings.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. The domain, hosting and website files are in your name or handed over to you. If we ever stop working together, you keep everything.

Can I edit the website myself?

We decide this before the build. If you want to update text, prices or photos often, I set it up so you can. If not, small updates can be part of a monthly plan.

Do you write the content?

I can write the page copy based on a call with you, or polish what you already have. Words written for your customers do more for enquiries and SEO than any design choice.

Do you build landing pages for ads?

Yes. A page built for one offer, matched to the ad that sends people there, usually converts far better than sending ad traffic to your homepage.

Should I hire a freelance web developer or an agency?

A freelance web developer suits most small and mid-sized businesses. You deal directly with the person building the site, and the cost is usually lower. An agency fits better when you need a large team, for example a big online store or several sites at once. Either way, the domain and hosting should be in your name.

Do you work as a freelance web developer for businesses outside Kerala?

Yes. I work over calls and WhatsApp with businesses across India and with Malayali-focused brands in the Gulf. The website, domain and hosting stay in your name.

Are you based in Trivandrum?

Yes. I’m based in Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram) and build websites for businesses across Kerala, and for Malayali-focused brands abroad, over calls and WhatsApp. If you’re in Trivandrum, we can meet in person.
