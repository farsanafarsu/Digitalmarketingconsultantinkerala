Search terms

The search terms report is where the money hides.

Google shows you the keywords you chose. The search terms report shows what people actually typed before clicking. In most accounts I audit, a big share of the budget goes to searches that were never going to become customers: job seekers, students, people wanting something free.

Reading that report every week and blocking the wrong searches is boring work. It is also the single biggest saving in most Kerala accounts, and it’s the first thing I do.

FAQ

Questions owners ask about Google ads in Kerala.

Who is the best Google Ads expert in Kerala for a small business?

The best one for you works in your own account, sets up conversion tracking before launch, reads the search terms every week and reports the cost per enquiry. Ask any expert, including me, to show you how they do each of these.

What is the difference between a Google Ads expert, specialist and PPC consultant?

Mostly the name. All three plan and manage paid search campaigns. PPC means pay per click, which is how most Google Search ads are charged. What matters is who does the work each week and what they report.

Are call ads still available on Google Ads?

New call ads can no longer be created, and Google says existing ones will stop showing in February 2027. The replacement is a responsive search ad with a call asset, which still puts a call button on your ad.

How much do Google ads cost in Kerala?

There are two parts: the ad budget you pay Google for clicks, and my management fee. What a click costs depends on your industry and town. A clinic or a training course in Kochi costs more per click than a niche service in a smaller town. I look at real bid estimates for your keywords in the audit before suggesting a budget.

What is the minimum budget for Google ads?

It has to be enough to get a steady number of clicks on your main keywords every day, otherwise the account can’t learn. After the audit I suggest a starting daily budget for your keywords and town, and we raise it only when the cost per enquiry is right.

Should I run Google ads or Meta ads?

Google ads reach people who are already searching for what you sell. Meta ads reach people before they search. If people search for your service, start with Google. If you need to create demand, start with Meta. Many Kerala businesses end up using both.

How soon will I get enquiries from Google ads?

Search ads can bring clicks on the first day. The first two to four weeks are for learning: checking search terms, adding negatives and testing ads. The cost per enquiry usually improves after that.

Do you set up conversion tracking?

Yes, always, and before launch. Calls from ads, calls from your website, forms and WhatsApp clicks are tracked as conversions, so Google learns from real enquiries and you can see what each one cost.

What are negative keywords?

Searches you don’t want to pay for, like "free", "jobs", "course" or "salary". I read the search terms report every week and block irrelevant ones, so your budget goes to people who might buy.

Should I use Performance Max?

Sometimes. It works well for businesses with good conversion tracking and enough data, especially e-commerce. Without guardrails it can spend on your own brand name and low-quality placements, so I set exclusions and watch where the money goes.

Do I need a landing page?

It helps a lot. Someone searching for one service should land on a page about that service, with a clear call button and form. If your site doesn’t have one, I can build a fast landing page for it.

Can you take over my existing account?

Yes. I start with an audit of what is running, keep what works and fix what is wasting money. Your account history stays, which helps performance.

Will I own my Google Ads account?

Yes. Campaigns run in your own Google Ads account, linked to your Analytics and Search Console. If we stop working together, everything stays with you.

Can you guarantee the top position on Google?

No one can honestly guarantee a position. Ad rank depends on your bid, ad quality and the competition at that moment. What I can promise is clean tracking, weekly checks and clear reporting.

Can you run ads for customers in the Gulf?

Yes. I can target Malayalis searching from the UAE, Qatar and Saudi Arabia for Kerala services such as property, interiors, education and healthcare, with separate campaigns and budgets per country.

Are you based in Trivandrum?

Yes. I’m based in Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram) and work with businesses across Kerala, and with Malayali-focused brands abroad, over calls and WhatsApp. For a Trivandrum business I can target the areas your customers come from, rather than the whole district.
