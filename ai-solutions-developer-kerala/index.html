How it works

How I build your AI, step by step.

Start small, prove it works on real enquiries, then grow it.

  1. Discover

    We find the repeated task that costs you the most time or the most leads.

  2. Gather your knowledge

    Prices, timings, FAQs, policies and past chats, so the AI answers from facts.

  3. Prototype

    A small working version on your real questions, so you can try it before anything goes live.

  4. Build and connect

    Connected to your website, WhatsApp, Google Sheets or CRM, with a handover to people.

  5. Test and launch

    Tested on real conversations, edge cases and Malayalam messages before launch.

  6. Review and improve

    I read the conversations each month and keep improving the answers.

FAQ

Questions owners ask about AI for business.

What can AI do for a small business in Kerala?

Most often: answer the same customer questions every day, reply to enquiries from your ads in seconds, ask the qualifying questions, book calls, send follow-ups on time, and turn messy data into clear reports. We start with the task that costs you the most time or the most leads.

How much does an AI chatbot cost in Kerala?

It depends on where it runs (website, WhatsApp or both), how many questions and flows it needs, what it connects to, and whether it uses a paid AI model per message. I quote a fixed build price after a short call, with any monthly running costs shown upfront.

Can the chatbot reply in Malayalam?

Yes. Chatbots can be set up to understand and reply in English, Malayalam and Manglish, depending on how your customers write to you.

Can it work on WhatsApp?

Yes. It can be connected to the WhatsApp Business Platform so enquiries from your ads get an instant reply, a few qualifying questions and a follow-up.

Will the chatbot make things up?

It shouldn’t, and I build it so it doesn’t. The bot answers from your own information, such as prices, timings, courses and policies. When it doesn’t know, it says so and hands the chat to a person.

Can a person take over the chat?

Yes. Every bot I build has a clear handover: hot leads, complaints and anything unusual go to you or your team, with the conversation so far.

What about customer data and privacy?

Only the details you need are collected, they are stored in your own sheet or CRM, and customers are told they’re chatting with an assistant. I’ll explain where data goes before anything goes live.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. I build and connect everything, and you get a simple way to see the leads and conversations.

How long does it take to build?

A focused website or WhatsApp assistant usually takes two to four weeks, including testing on real questions. Custom machine learning projects take longer and start with a small proof of concept.

Can you build custom machine learning models?

Yes. I build models in Python with libraries such as NumPy, Pandas, OpenCV and NLTK, for tasks like predicting, classifying, scoring or reading images and text, using your own data.

How does this connect with my marketing?

Your ads bring the enquiries; the AI answers them straight away, asks the right questions and passes ready leads to you. That way less of your ad budget is wasted on leads that go cold.

Are you based in Trivandrum?

Yes. I’m based in Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram) and work with businesses across Kerala, and with Malayali-focused brands abroad, over calls and WhatsApp. If you’re in Trivandrum, we can meet in person.
