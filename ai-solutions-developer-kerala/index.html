FAQ

Questions about AI solutions

What can AI do for a small business?

Most often: answer the same customer questions every day, qualify enquiries from your ads, send follow-ups on time and turn messy data into clear reports. We start with the task that costs you the most.

Can the chatbot reply in Malayalam?

Yes. Chatbots can be set up to understand and reply in English and Malayalam, depending on how your customers write to you.

Can it work on WhatsApp?

Yes. It can be connected to WhatsApp Business so enquiries from your ads get an instant reply and a follow-up.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. I build and connect everything, and you get a simple way to see the leads and conversations.

How does this connect with my marketing?

Your ads bring the enquiries; the AI answers them straight away, asks the right questions and passes ready leads to you. That way less of your ad budget is wasted on leads that go cold.
