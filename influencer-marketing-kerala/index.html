Before you pay a creator

Mistakes I fix in most influencer campaigns.

Influencer campaigns usually fail before the reel is posted. These are the checks I run, and how each creator is measured.

Choosing by follower count

Big numbers can hide bought followers or an audience outside Kerala. I check engagement, comments and where the audience lives.

A vague brief

Creators need the offer, the must-say points and the freedom to sound like themselves. A clear brief saves rounds of edits.

No way to track who sold

Every creator gets their own link or code, so enquiries and sales are counted per creator, not guessed.

Paying everyone upfront

I start with a small test batch, then put the budget behind the creators whose audience actually responded.

Proven at scale

For Parleg’s Onam campaign I coordinated 50+ nano, micro and macro influencers, from outreach and briefs to approvals and publishing, in two weeks.

Measured per creator

Views, link clicks or code uses, enquiries and cost per enquiry for each creator. The best reels can then run as Meta partnership ads.

FAQ

Questions about influencer marketing

How many influencers do you work with?

My influencer dashboard has 10,000+ creators across every niche, so the shortlist starts from people who already reach your kind of customer.

Do you work with micro influencers?

Yes. For many local Kerala businesses, a few micro influencers with an engaged local audience bring more enquiries than one big name.

How do you check a creator is genuine?

I look at their reach, views and audience before you pay, to avoid accounts with inflated followers or the wrong audience.

How do you measure results?

Each creator gets their own tracking link or code, so you can see how many enquiries each one brought in.

Can the creator content be used as ads?

Yes. With the right usage rights agreed in the brief, the best reels can run as Meta ads to reach more people.

Are you based in Trivandrum?

Yes. I’m based in Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram) and work with businesses across Kerala, and with Malayali-focused brands abroad, over calls and WhatsApp. I work with creators from every district, not just the capital.
