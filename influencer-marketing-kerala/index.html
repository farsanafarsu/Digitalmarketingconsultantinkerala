Why influencer campaigns fail

Most influencer campaigns fail before the Reel is posted.

The problem is rarely the creator. It’s who was picked, what they were told, and whether anyone was counting. Four I see often:

Week 1 · Big name

4.8 lakh followers · ₹60,000

A jewellery brand books the biggest name it can afford. The Reel gets likes from all over India.

Two enquiries from Kerala.

Week 2 · Audience check

Top audience: Indonesia, Brazil

A gym in Kochi pays a fitness creator with a huge following. Nobody checked where the followers live.

Most of them were never coming to Kochi.

Launch day · 12 creators

No links, no codes

A café in Kozhikode invites twelve creators for its opening. The place is full for a week.

Nobody knows which creator brought whom.

Onam · posted on Uthradam

“Sorry, editing took time”

A saree shop’s festival collaboration goes live the day before Onam, after most families have already shopped.

Paid in full. Posted too late.

Followers or reach

Follower count is the least useful number.

Both creators charge the same. Creator A has ten times the followers, but most of them are outside Kerala and few watch the Reels. Creator B is smaller, with an audience that lives where your customers live and actually comments.

That’s why every shortlist I make is checked on views, engagement and audience location before you pay. A smaller creator in the right town usually beats a big one everywhere else.

Pricing

How much does influencer marketing cost in Kerala?

You pay two things: creator fees, and a fee for planning and managing the campaign. Creator fees vary widely, from free products for nano creators to large fees for well-known names.

I start with your goal and budget, build a shortlist with expected costs, and suggest a test batch. Budget only grows behind the creators whose audiences actually responded.

What moves the price

  • Creator tierNano and micro creators cost far less than macro names.
  • DeliverablesOne Reel, Reels plus stories, or a visit and multiple posts.
  • Usage rightsUsing the creator’s Reel as an ad usually costs extra.
  • Number of creatorsTen micro creators or one big name, for the same budget.
  • TimingOnam, Christmas and wedding season are the busiest and priciest.

FAQ

Questions owners ask about influencer marketing.

How much does influencer marketing cost in Kerala?

Two parts: what you pay creators, and a fee for planning and managing the campaign. Creator fees depend on their tier, the deliverables (a Reel, stories, a visit), and whether you want to use their content as ads. I build a budget after the free audit, with a test batch first.

How many influencers do you work with?

My influencer dashboard has 10,000+ creators across every niche, so the shortlist starts from people who already reach your kind of customer.

Micro or macro influencers: which is better?

For most local Kerala businesses, a few micro influencers with an engaged local audience bring more enquiries than one big name. Macro creators make sense for launches and brand awareness across the state. Many campaigns mix both.

How do you check a creator is genuine?

I look at their real reach, views per Reel, comments and where their audience lives before you pay. That filters out inflated followers and audiences outside Kerala.

How do you measure results?

Each creator gets their own tracking link, code or WhatsApp message, so you can see views, clicks, enquiries and cost per enquiry for each one.

Can the creator content be used as ads?

Yes. With usage rights agreed in the brief, the best Reels can run as Meta partnership ads, which usually perform better than brand-made ads.

Do you only work with Malayalam creators?

Mostly, for Kerala audiences. For brands selling to the rest of India or to Malayalis in the Gulf, I also work with creators in English, Tamil and other languages, or Gulf-based Malayali creators.

How long does a campaign take?

From brief to first posts, usually two to three weeks. For Parleg’s Onam campaign I coordinated 50+ influencers, from outreach to publishing, in two weeks.

Do I need to send free products?

For product brands, yes, creators need to try what they review. For services such as clinics, restaurants or institutes, a visit or experience is arranged instead.

Who writes the script?

The brief sets the offer, the must-say points and what to avoid. The creator writes in their own voice, because their audience follows them for how they talk. I review the draft before it goes live.

What about disclosure rules?

Paid posts are marked as paid partnerships or ads, as Indian advertising guidelines expect. It protects you and the creator, and audiences trust honest collaborations more.

Are you based in Trivandrum?

Yes. I’m based in Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram) and work with businesses across Kerala, and with Malayali-focused brands abroad, over calls and WhatsApp. I work with creators from every district, not just the capital.
