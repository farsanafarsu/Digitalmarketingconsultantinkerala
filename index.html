—How to choose

How to choose the best digital marketing consultant in Kerala

Plenty of people call themselves the best digital marketing consultant in Kerala. Five checks will tell you who is worth your money.

  1. 01

    Ask for real numbers.

    Screenshots from Ads Manager or Search Console, not adjectives.

  2. 02

    Ask who does the work.

    In many agencies the person who sells to you never touches your account. With me, the person you talk to is the person running it.

  3. 03

    Check they understand tracking.

    If they can’t explain how a lead gets counted, their reports don’t mean much.

  4. 04

    Look for local knowledge.

    Kerala buyers search in Malayalam, Manglish and English, and most would sooner send a WhatsApp message than fill in a form.

  5. 05

    Start small.

    A consultant who believes in the plan will suggest a test budget before a long contract.

—FAQ

Questions people ask before hiring a digital marketing consultant in Kerala

How much does a digital marketing consultant in Kerala charge?

It depends on the channels and the size of the ad budget. My management fee starts at Rs X a month. Ad spend is separate and you pay it directly to Meta or Google. The first audit is free.

Who is the best digital marketing consultant in Kerala for a small business?

The best one for you has run campaigns in your kind of business, can show real results and picks up when you call. Ask for case studies and a small test before you commit to anyone, including me.

Should I hire a consultant or a digital marketing agency?

An agency suits large brands that need a big team. A consultant suits owners who want one accountable person, lower cost and quicker decisions.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Local keywords often move in two to three months. Competitive state-wide keywords usually take four to six months or longer. Ads can bring leads in the first week while SEO builds.

What budget do I need for Meta ads or Google ads?

Most local businesses can run a useful test with Rs X a day. If your budget is too small to learn anything, I’ll say so in the audit.

Which is better for my business, Meta ads or Google ads?

Google ads catches people already searching for what you sell. Meta ads reaches people who would want it but aren’t searching yet. Most businesses do best with both, starting with the one that fits how their customers buy.

Does influencer marketing work for small businesses in Kerala?

Yes, when the creator’s followers are your customers. A micro influencer in your own town often brings more enquiries than a celebrity page, at a fraction of the cost. I track each creator separately so you know what you got for the money.

Can you work with my existing website and ad account?

Yes. I start with what you have, fix the tracking first, and rebuild only what is holding results back.

What are AEO and GEO?

They are ways of preparing your website so AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Google’s AI overviews can quote and recommend your business when people ask them questions.

Do you work with businesses outside Kerala?

Yes. I work with brands in other states and the Gulf that want to reach Malayali customers.
