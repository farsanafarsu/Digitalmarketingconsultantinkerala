FAQ

Questions about Google ads

Should I run Google ads or Meta ads?

Google ads reach people who are already searching for what you sell. Meta ads reach people before they search. Many Kerala businesses use both; the free audit helps decide where to start.

How much budget do I need for Google ads?

It depends on how competitive your searches are in your town. I look at that during the audit and suggest a starting budget you can grow from.

Do you set up conversion tracking?

Yes, always, and before launch. Calls, forms and WhatsApp clicks are tracked so you can see what each lead cost.

What are negative keywords?

Searches you do not want to pay for, like “free” or “jobs”. I check the search terms report and block irrelevant ones so your budget goes to real customers.

Can you take over my existing account?

Yes. I start with an audit of what is running, keep what works and fix what is wasting money.
