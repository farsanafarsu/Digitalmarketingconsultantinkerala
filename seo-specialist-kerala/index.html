Before you start

Mistakes I fix on most Kerala websites.

Most sites I audit are not short of content. They have a few technical and structural problems that stop Google from trusting them.

Pages Google can’t index

A stray noindex tag, a blocked folder or a page missing from the sitemap. I check indexing first, because nothing else matters until it is fixed.

One page trying to rank for everything

A homepage can’t rank for every service in every town. Each service gets its own page with its own search intent.

An incomplete Google Business Profile

Wrong categories, no services listed and unanswered reviews cost you map results. I fill in every section and keep it active.

Copy-paste town pages

Dozens of near-identical city pages look like spam to Google. I only build a location page when there is something genuinely local to say.

Measured in Search Console

Impressions, clicks, average position and the queries that found you, from Google Search Console, not a third-party guess.

And in enquiries

Calls, direction requests and forms that came from organic search, so you can see what the rankings are worth.

FAQ

Questions about SEO

How long does SEO take to work?

Technical fixes can help within weeks, but rankings usually build over a few months. That is why I start with the fixes that matter most and report progress every month.

What is in your SEO audit?

A 65 to 66 point technical and on-page check across 11 categories, from indexability and speed to images, links and structured data. Every issue is ranked by priority, with a fix for each.

Do you do local SEO for Kerala towns?

Yes. I set up and improve your Google Business Profile, local listings and town pages so you show up when people nearby search for what you sell.

What are AEO and GEO?

Answer engine optimisation and generative engine optimisation: structuring your pages, FAQs and schema so AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Google’s AI overviews can understand your business and recommend it.

Can you fix my site or only audit it?

Both. The audit tells you what is wrong; I can then fix it myself or work with your developer.

Are you based in Trivandrum?

Yes. I’m based in Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram) and work with businesses across Kerala, and with Malayali-focused brands abroad, over calls and WhatsApp. Local SEO for Trivandrum businesses is a big part of that work.

Can you guarantee a first-page ranking?

No. Anyone who guarantees rankings is guessing, because Google decides. I guarantee the work: a full audit, fixes done properly, and a monthly report from Search Console so you can see the progress yourself.
