SEO services in Kerala

Everything your site needs to rank and convert.

Nine pieces of work, done by one person who understands how they fit together.

Technical SEO

Crawling, indexing, site speed, Core Web Vitals, mobile layout, redirects, canonical tags, sitemaps and structured data. Nothing else works until Google can read and trust your site, so this comes first.

On-page SEO

Titles, meta descriptions, headings, copy and internal links written around how your customers actually search. One clear page per service, each matched to one search intent, so pages stop competing with each other.

Local SEO and Google Business Profile

Categories, services, photos, posts, Q&A and review replies, plus consistent name, address and phone across listings. This is what gets you into the map pack for “near me” searches in your town.

Keyword research in English, Malayalam and Manglish

The real words people in Kerala type, sorted by intent: who is researching, who is comparing and who is ready to call. Manglish searches are often the easiest to win, because few competitors target them.

Content that answers buyers

Service pages, location pages and guides that answer the questions customers ask before they buy: price, process, timelines, proof. Written for people first, then structured for Google.

AEO and GEO for AI answers

FAQs, schema, clear entity details and cited facts, so ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google’s AI Overviews can understand what you do, where you are and who you serve.

Website SEO audit

My 65 to 66 point check across 11 categories, with every issue ranked by priority and the fix written out. Useful on its own, or as the first month of ongoing SEO.

Reporting you can read

Each month: impressions, clicks, average position and top searches from Google Search Console, calls and direction requests from your business profile, and enquiries from search. Plus what I’m doing next.

Links and digital PR

Mentions in Kerala business directories, local media, associations and partner sites, earned rather than bought. A few relevant links beat hundreds of random ones.

AI search

ChatGPT, Gemini and AI Overviews: what actually helps.

More people now ask an AI assistant before they search. That has created a wave of “AI SEO” packages that promise a mention in ChatGPT. Nobody can promise that, because nobody controls those answers.

What we do know: Google says its AI Overviews need nothing beyond normal SEO best practice, and other AI tools lean heavily on pages that already rank well and are clearly written. So the work is the same good SEO, done more carefully.

What helps

  • Pages that answer one question clearly, near the top
  • FAQ and organisation schema that state who you are, where, and what you do
  • Consistent business details everywhere you are listed
  • Reviews and mentions on sites that AI tools already trust
  • Facts, prices and processes written out, not hidden in images

What’s being sold anyway

  • “Guaranteed ChatGPT ranking”
  • Special files or tags that AI tools are said to obey
  • Hundreds of AI-written pages targeting every town
  • Paid “AI directories” that nobody reads

FAQ

Questions owners ask about SEO in Kerala.

How long does SEO take to work in Kerala?

Technical fixes can show up in Search Console within a few weeks, once Google recrawls the pages. Rankings for competitive searches usually build over three to six months. Local map results often move sooner, because a complete Google Business Profile is a strong signal on its own.

Can you guarantee a first-page ranking?

No, and nobody honestly can. Google says so in its own guide to hiring an SEO. What I can guarantee is the work: a full audit, fixes done properly, and a monthly report from your own Search Console so you can check the progress yourself.

What is included in your SEO audit?

A 65 to 66 point technical and on-page check across 11 categories, including indexability, speed, mobile, titles and headings, images, links and structured data. Every issue is ranked by priority, with the fix written out, so you or your developer can act on it.

Should I hire an SEO expert or an SEO company in Kerala?

It depends on what you need. A company can run many services at once. An independent expert means the person who audits your site is the person who fixes it and reports to you. For most small and mid-sized Kerala businesses, direct access matters more than headcount.

How much does SEO cost in Kerala?

It depends on the size of your site, how competitive your searches are, how many locations you serve and how much content is needed. I quote a fixed monthly scope after the free audit, so the price is tied to the actual work your site needs.

Do you do local SEO for my town?

Yes. I set up and improve your Google Business Profile, clean up your listings and build location pages only where there is something genuinely local to say. That covers Trivandrum, Kochi, Kozhikode, Thrissur and the rest of Kerala.

Can you help me rank in the Gulf as well as Kerala?

Yes. Many Kerala businesses sell to Malayalis in the UAE, Qatar and Saudi Arabia. I research the searches those buyers make and make sure the right pages are set up for those countries, without hurting your ranking at home.

Do you do keyword research in Malayalam?

Yes. People in Kerala search in English, in Malayalam script and in Manglish. I check all three, because a search like the one your customer types in Manglish often has less competition.

What are AEO and GEO?

Answer engine optimisation and generative engine optimisation. It means structuring your pages, FAQs and schema so AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Google’s AI Overviews can understand your business and cite it. Nobody can promise an AI mention, but a clear, well-structured site gives you the best chance.

Will SEO work for a new website?

Yes, but it takes longer, because Google has no history to trust yet. For a new site I focus first on a clean technical setup, a complete Google Business Profile and a small number of strong service pages, rather than a lot of thin content.

Do I need to redesign my website for SEO?

Usually not. Most sites I audit need fixes, not a rebuild. If a redesign is already planned, bring me in before it launches so the old pages redirect properly and you don’t lose the rankings you already have.

Can you fix my site or only audit it?

Both. The audit tells you what is wrong. I can then make the fixes myself or work alongside your developer, whichever is quicker for your setup.

How will I know if SEO is working?

Every month you get impressions, clicks, average position and the searches that found you, from Google Search Console, plus the calls, direction requests and form enquiries that came from search. If a number goes down, the report says why and what I’m doing about it.

Are you based in Trivandrum?

Yes. I’m based in Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram) and work with businesses across Kerala, and with Malayali-focused brands abroad, over calls and WhatsApp. If you’re in Trivandrum, we can meet in person.
