Where the money goes

Most Kerala businesses don’t lose money on bad ads.

They lose it on small things around the ads: the wrong campaign type, a weak form, a slow reply, a tired creative. Four I see every month:

Week 1 · Boosted post

A furniture showroom in Thrissur boosts its best photo. The likes pour in from all over India. The phone stays quiet.

Zero calls.

Week 3 · Lead form

Instant form · 300 leads at ₹18

A training institute in Kochi celebrates cheap leads. The counsellor calls all 300. Most don’t remember filling the form.

Forty picked up.

Friday, 8:30 pm · WhatsApp

Click-to-WhatsApp · 26 new chats

A clinic in Kollam runs a weekend offer. The chats arrive after the front desk has gone home.

Nobody replied till Monday.

Month 3 · Same creative

Frequency 6.2 · cost per lead up 3×

A builder in Trivandrum has run the same ad since launch. The same people have seen it six times. Results quietly fall.

Nobody noticed.

Cost per lead

The cheapest lead is rarely the best one.

Both campaigns spend ₹2,000. Campaign A looks better in Ads Manager: more leads, a third of the price. But only a few of those people pick up, and fewer buy. Campaign B costs three times more per lead and brings in more than three times the sales.

That is why I agree the real goal with you before launch, ask how the leads are converting every week, and move the budget on sales, not just on cost per lead.

How it works

How I run your Meta ads, step by step.

The same six steps for every account, whether you spend a little or a lot.

  1. Audit

    Your ad account, Pixel, past campaigns and competitors’ ads, checked for where money is being lost.

  2. Tracking

    Pixel, Conversions API, WhatsApp and call tracking set up and tested, so every result is counted.

  3. Offer and creative

    The offer, the hook and three or four creatives to test, in the language your customers use.

  4. Launch and test

    Campaigns go live with audiences and creatives running side by side for a fair test.

  5. Optimise

    Weak ads paused, strong ones scaled, new creatives added before the old ones wear out.

  6. Report and scale

    A weekly report on what each lead cost and how they converted, and budget moved to what works.

FAQ

Questions owners ask about Meta ads in Kerala.

How much do Facebook and Instagram ads cost in Kerala?

There are two costs: your ad budget, paid to Meta, and my management fee. On the accounts I have managed, calls from ads have cost from about ₹12 each and WhatsApp conversations from about ₹19 to ₹116 each, depending on the business and the town. I quote the management fee after the free audit.

What is the minimum budget for Meta ads?

You can start small, but the budget has to be enough for Meta to learn who enquires. After the audit I suggest a starting daily budget for your goal and town, and we only raise it on campaigns that bring leads at a cost you are happy with.

Should I boost posts or run proper campaigns?

Run campaigns. The Boost button optimises for likes and views. A leads, messages or calls campaign tells Meta to find people who actually enquire, and gives you far more control over audience, placement and creative.

Do you run click-to-WhatsApp and call ads?

Yes. Most of my campaigns for Kerala businesses are lead forms, click-to-WhatsApp ads or call ads, because they bring enquiries you can act on the same day.

How soon will I see leads?

Usually within the first few days of launch. The first two to three weeks are a testing period while Meta learns and we compare creatives and audiences. Cost per lead normally settles after that.

Why are my leads cheap but low quality?

Usually because the form is too easy, the audience is too broad or the ad promises something vague. I add qualifying questions, tighten the offer and judge campaigns on how many leads actually pick up and buy, not just on cost per lead.

Do you set up the Meta Pixel and Conversions API?

Yes. Without them Meta can’t see who became a customer on your website, so it can’t find more people like them. I install both, test the events and make sure purchases or enquiries are counted once.

Can you target customers in Malayalam?

Yes. I research and test audiences in Malayalam and English, and write ad copy in the language your customers use, including Manglish where it fits.

Do you make the ad creatives?

I plan the creatives: the offer, the hook, the format and what to test. I can write the copy and brief your designer or videographer, or work with the photos and videos you already have.

Will I own my ad account and data?

Yes. Campaigns run in your own Business Manager and ad account, with your Pixel and your audiences. If we ever stop working together, everything stays with you.

How will I know if the ads are working?

Every week you get spend, results, cost per result, reach and frequency from Ads Manager, plus what I changed and what I’m testing next. We agree before launch what counts as a result.

Do Meta ads work for e-commerce and product sellers?

They can. For Trenster, an e-commerce store in Qatar, nine product and combo campaigns brought 604 WhatsApp and Messenger conversations at about ₹18.72 each. The full numbers are in the Trenster case study.

Do you run ads for Malayalis in the Gulf?

Yes. I run Meta ads for brands in Dubai, Sharjah and Qatar, and for Kerala businesses that sell to NRIs, such as builders, interior designers and colleges.

Are you based in Trivandrum?

Yes. I’m based in Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram) and work with businesses across Kerala, and with Malayali-focused brands abroad, over calls and WhatsApp. If you’re in Trivandrum, we can meet in person.
