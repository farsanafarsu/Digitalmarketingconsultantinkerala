Before you spend more

Mistakes I fix in most Meta ad accounts.

Most accounts I audit are losing money in the same few places. These are what I check first, and how I measure the fix.

Boosting posts instead of running campaigns

The Boost button chases likes and views. A leads, messages or calls campaign tells Meta to find people who actually enquire.

No Pixel or Conversions API

Without them Meta can’t see who became a customer, so it can’t find more people like them. I install and test both.

Judging ads by cost per lead alone

A ₹20 lead that never picks up costs more than a ₹60 lead that books. I check lead quality with you, not just the price.

One creative running for months

When frequency climbs, results drop. I rotate new images, videos and offers before the old ones wear out.

A result agreed before launch

We decide upfront what counts: a WhatsApp conversation, a call or a form. That is the number every report is built on.

Reports with the Ads Manager numbers

Spend, results, cost per result, reach and frequency, straight from Ads Manager, with a note on what I’m changing next.

FAQ

Questions about Meta ads

How much should I spend on Meta ads?

It depends on your goal and your town. After the free audit I suggest a starting budget, then we raise it only on the campaigns that bring leads at a cost you are happy with.

Do you run click-to-WhatsApp and call ads?

Yes. Most of my campaigns for Kerala businesses are lead forms, click-to-WhatsApp ads or call ads, because those bring enquiries you can act on the same day.

Can you target customers in Malayalam?

Yes. I research and test audiences in both Malayalam and English, and write ad copy in the language your customers use.

How will I know if the ads are working?

You get a weekly report showing spend, leads and the cost of each lead, with the Ads Manager numbers behind it.

Do you work with businesses outside Kerala?

Yes. I also run Meta ads for brands in Dubai, Sharjah, Qatar and Canada that want to reach Malayali customers.

Are you based in Trivandrum?

Yes. I’m based in Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram) and work with businesses across Kerala, and with Malayali-focused brands abroad, over calls and WhatsApp.

Do Meta ads work for product sellers and e-commerce?

They can. For Trenster, an e-commerce store in Qatar, nine product and combo campaigns brought 604 WhatsApp and Messenger conversations at about ₹18.72 each. The full numbers are in the Trenster case study.
