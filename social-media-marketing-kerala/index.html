FAQ

Questions owners ask about social media in Kerala.

What does a social media marketing expert do?

They plan your content, create and publish posts and Reels, reply to comments and messages, support the best posts with ads when useful, and report which content brought enquiries.

Who is the best social media marketing expert in Kerala for a small business?

The best one for you can show pages they have grown, explains the plan before posting, writes in your customers’ language, never buys followers and reports messages as well as reach.

Can one person manage my Instagram and Facebook?

Yes, for most small and mid-sized businesses. Content can be planned once and adapted for both. Brands that need daily shoots or many platforms may need a larger team.

How much does social media marketing cost in Kerala?

It depends on how many platforms, how many posts and Reels a month, whether shoots are needed, and whether ads run alongside. I quote a fixed monthly scope after the free audit, so you know exactly what is included.

Which platforms do you manage?

Mostly Instagram and Facebook, because that is where most Kerala customers are. I add YouTube Shorts or LinkedIn when your customers are there, and I’d rather do two platforms well than five badly.

How many posts a month do I need?

Fewer than you think, if each one has a purpose. For most local businesses, two or three Reels a week plus stories every day works better than a post every day with no plan.

Do you create the content or only post it?

I plan the content, write the captions and edit short videos. For shoots I work with your team or a photographer, and I brief creators when the plan needs them.

Can you post in Malayalam?

Yes. I write in Malayalam, English or a mix, depending on how your customers talk.

How long before social media brings enquiries?

Organic growth builds over months, not weeks. If you need enquiries sooner, I run a small Meta ads campaign alongside the content so the best posts reach the right people.

Do you reply to comments and DMs?

Yes. Comments and messages are answered quickly in your brand’s voice, and enquiries are passed to you or your sales team the same day.

Will you grow my followers?

Yes, but followers are not the goal. I aim content at people in your area who could become customers, and measure profile visits, saves, shares and messages as closely as follower count.

Do you buy followers or use engagement pods?

Never. Bought followers don’t buy anything, and they hurt your reach because Instagram shows your posts to people who never engage.

What do I need to give you?

Access to your accounts, your offers and prices, and an hour a month to approve the calendar. If you can share photos or short clips from your shop, clinic or team, the content will feel far more real.

How do you report results?

Every month: reach, follower growth, saves, shares, profile visits, website clicks and the messages that came from content, with what I’ll change next month.

Are you based in Trivandrum?

Yes. I’m based in Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram) and work with businesses across Kerala, and with Malayali-focused brands abroad, over calls and WhatsApp. For Trivandrum brands I can be there for shoots.
