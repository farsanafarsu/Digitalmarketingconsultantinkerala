Before you post

Mistakes I fix on most business pages.

Most pages aren’t short of posts. They are short of a plan and a reason for anyone to follow.

Posting without a plan

Random posts when there is time. A calendar with a purpose for each post is what builds a following.

Chasing follower count

Followers who will never buy don’t help. I aim content at people in your area who could become customers.

Unanswered messages

A slow reply to a DM is a lost customer. Messages get answered and every enquiry is passed on.

FAQ

Questions about social media marketing

Which platforms do you manage?

Mostly Instagram and Facebook, because that is where most Kerala customers are. I add YouTube Shorts or LinkedIn when your customers are there.

Do you create the content or only post it?

I plan the content, write the captions and edit short videos. For shoots I work with your team or a photographer, and I brief creators when the plan needs them.

How long before social media brings enquiries?

Organic growth builds over months, not weeks. If you need enquiries sooner, I run a small Meta ads campaign alongside the content so the best posts reach the right people.

Can you post in Malayalam?

Yes. I write in Malayalam, English or a mix, depending on how your customers talk.

Are you based in Trivandrum?

Yes. I’m based in Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram) and work with businesses across Kerala, and with Malayali-focused brands abroad, over calls and WhatsApp.
